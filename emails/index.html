Mentorship emails

Open a message to see its HTML format. All examples are fictional. Links use example.invalid and cannot submit answers. The same layout is used by outgoing email, with a plain-text alternative.

  1. Applications open

    Prospective participants · Chair-approved invitation; not automatic.
    Subject: Example Chapter Mentorship applications

  2. Application received

    Applicant · Automatic receipt after an application is saved.
    Subject: We received your mentorship application

  3. Moving to matching

    Applicant · Chair-approved message.
    Subject: Your Example Chapter Mentorship application

  4. Not selected

    Applicant · Chair-approved message.
    Subject: Your Example Chapter Mentorship application

  5. Mentor invitation

    Prospective mentor · Chair-approved invitation.
    Subject: Invitation to mentor an Example Chapter member

  6. Match introduction

    Matched participants · Chair personalizes and approves the introduction.
    Subject: Your Example Chapter mentorship introduction

  7. Training invitation

    Selected participants · Chair fills in the meeting details and approves sending.
    Subject: Required Example Chapter mentorship training

  8. New mentee application

    Chair · Automatic notice after an application is saved.
    Subject: New mentorship application

  9. New mentor application

    Chair · Automatic notice after an application is saved.
    Subject: New mentorship application

  10. First meeting confirmation

    Mentee · Day after the booked meeting. The same wording is repeated on days 7 and 14 if unanswered.
    Subject: Your first mentoring meeting

  11. Month 3 check-in

    Mentee and mentor · Automatic, measured from confirmed first attendance.
    Subject: Your Example Chapter mentorship check-in

  12. Month 6 check-in

  13. Month 9 check-in

    Subject: Your Example Chapter mentorship check-in

    Subject: Your Example Chapter mentorship cycle

  15. Early ending: mentee

    mentee · Final feedback. Each role receives its own questions.
    Subject: Your Example Chapter mentorship cycle

  16. Month 12: mentor

    mentor · Final feedback. Each role receives its own questions.
    Subject: Your Example Chapter mentorship cycle

  17. Early ending: mentor

    Subject: Your Example Chapter mentorship cycle

    Participant who has not replied · Automatic for quarterly and final check-ins. First-meeting reminders use the confirmation above.
    Subject: A reminder about your mentorship check-in

  19. Day 14 reminder

    Participant who has not replied · Automatic for quarterly and final check-ins. First-meeting reminders use the confirmation above.
    Subject: Your mentorship feedback
