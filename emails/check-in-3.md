# Your Example Chapter mentorship check-in

It’s been three months since your first mentoring meeting. We’d like to hear how things are going.

Please reply with your answers to these questions:

1. How many times have you met since your mentorship began?
2. What value, if any, have you received from the mentorship?
3. Would you like the Mentorship Chair to contact you?

There is no need to share the details of your mentoring conversations.

Thank you for being part of the program and helping us make it better.
